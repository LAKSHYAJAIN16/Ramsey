01

Choose it on your desktop

Ramsey races three candidate sources at once through Browserbase. First valid recipe wins — the rest get cancelled, browsers and all.

  • Deterministic JSON-LD parse first, ~1 second
  • Real Stagehand browser fallback if that fails
  • Junk extractions auto-rejected, never shown

02

Pair your Quest and get cooking

An installed Unity app displays your desktop recipe in Quest passthrough. Camera checks can advance visibly completed steps; you stay in control with Next and Back.

  • Durations auto-detected from the recipe text
  • Desktop and headset share one cooking session
  • Recent dishes and shareable links, no retyping
"I'm out of garlic, and double it"
Right — swapped for shallot, servings doubled. Keep going.
03

Double tap. Ask your chef.

Double tap the table to open Ramsey, a cube-shaped voice assistant for this prototype. Ask for help, read your transcription, and hear a spoken reply. Voice needs a connected desktop and available AI services.

  • Blunt, encouraging persona — never a real voice
  • "What Ramsey knows about you" memory panel
  • Plating feedback and points for completed recipes
